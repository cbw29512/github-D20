D&D 5e (2024) compatible combat simulation system. Load the cards, roll initiative, and watch a rules-driven fight play out.

Built for the 2024 rules. D&D 5e 2014 rules support is planned.

Simulation note: Iron Pit targets D&D 5e (2024) / SRD 5.2.1 compatibility, not exact tabletop emulation. D&D combat includes edge cases, optional interpretations, and DM rulings that do not map cleanly to automated play, so some complex interactions are intentionally simplified for consistent simulation. Results are a close representation of combat—not a replacement for the official rules, a DM's ruling, or play at the table.
